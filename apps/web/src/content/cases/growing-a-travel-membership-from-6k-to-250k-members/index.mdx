## The Challenge

A beloved but niche membership product had plateaued. Growth required rethinking
acquisition, retention, infrastructure, and the data that informed every decision —
without breaking what members already loved.

## The Solution

We embedded as the product and engineering team: rebuilt core flows for conversion,
hardened infrastructure to absorb 500k+ weekly pageviews with 900k peaks, instrumented
a privacy-respecting data platform, and ran a continuous experiment program against revenue.

## The Results

Membership grew roughly **40×**, from about 6,000 members in 2018 to **more than 250,000** by
2023, while we built and maintained the platform. The business was carried through multiple
acquisitions that integrated software, data, and users into a larger portfolio, and in 2026 it
was acquired by a public hospitality company for about **$130M**.
