When I founded Quarry in 2016, I wanted to create a web development firm that did things differently and truly understood businesses across various industries. An external firm that operated as seamlessly as an in-house dev team – with better results and a deeper bench of experts.

Our team has worked with everyone from e-commerce startups to major financial institutions, and what sets us apart is our experience – both as an external partner and working alongside in-house teams.

## The Problem with an Internal Team vs. External Team

We all know the feeling: You’re staring at your product roadmap, that knot in your stomach growing. Your dev team is maxed out, deadlines are looming, and your competitor just launched that feature you’ve been planning for months. We’ve all been there – that moment when you realize you need more hands on deck, but the thought of hiring, budgeting, and training makes your head spin.

It’s a question that often leaves organizations torn between building an in-house team from the ground up or hiring external developers. (And yes, concerns about cost, quality, and project timelines tend to dominate these discussions.)

But there are other issues here.

Admit it – you’re not sure you can trust an external vendor. There is a lot to fear – loss of control over the development process, unknown costs, no visibility into the day-to-day, and maybe worst of all – the external teams won’t truly understand your company culture and vision.

While the desire to maintain oversight of your dev process is strong – there are many hidden costs to building an in-house team.

From hiring and training to equipment and benefits, in-house teams are expensive. Plus, they often struggle to maintain cutting-edge skills and can’t scale quickly when needed. Your roadmap becomes inflexible, making it hard to pivot with market demands.

## What Happens When Things Go Sideways?

Not long ago, we encountered a mid-sized e-commerce company planning to revamp their website and add new features. They were at a crossroads, faced with hiring a team or outsourcing with Quarry.

Initially, they decided to build an in-house development team. They were excited to have full control over the process and felt like the in-person collaboration would go a long way.

But, they quickly ran into hurdles. The hiring process dragged on for months. Finding candidates with the right mix of skills proved challenging, and costs started mounting in unexpected ways.

As project timelines began to slip, the CEO grew increasingly concerned. Features they had hoped to launch were still in the planning stages and they were falling behind the competition.

That’s when they reached out to Quarry. We quickly assembled a selection of our fractional experts, tailored to their specific needs. Within weeks, we had the project back on track. We brought in specialists for each aspect of the development, from UX design to backend integration.

The result? The site launched on time with all planned features intact – leading to a significant boost in revenue and the project paid for itself, even those costly mistakes at the beginning.

## The Power of Quarry’s External Team

That fear of losing control? We get it. That’s why we’ve built our entire process around transparency and collaboration. With us you’re getting:

And Quarry is able to deliver at every stage of the product lifecycle, from planning through delivery and maintenance.
