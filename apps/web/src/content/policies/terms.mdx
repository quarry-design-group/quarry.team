## 6. Security and Maintenance

**6.1 Two categories.** Security and maintenance work on a Client Product falls into one of two categories. Critical Remediation Events are handled under the standing authorization in Section 6.4. Periodic Maintenance is handled at Client’s discretion under Section 6.6. This Section applies only to a Client Product, or the part of one, whose software maintenance is within the scope of the engagement, whether that scope is set by an Accepted SOW or evidenced as Section 2 provides. Access to, or deploy rights on, a Client Product without maintenance responsibility does not bring it within this Section: Quarry has no duty or authorization under this Section for systems it is not maintaining, and Section 7 governs their security.

**6.2 Critical Remediation Events.** A “Critical Remediation Event” is any of the following, where the vulnerability affects software, a configuration, or a dependency present in the Client Product:

- a vulnerability with a CVSS base score of 8.0 or higher under any CVSS version, using the highest base score published by NVD, the vendor, or the assigning CVE Numbering Authority, or a vendor-assigned severity of Critical;
- a vulnerability of any score that appears in the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, that a vendor or credible security advisory reports as actively exploited, or for which a public exploit exists and Quarry reasonably believes the Client Product is exposed;
- a vulnerability with a CVSS base score of 7.0 to 7.9, or a vendor-assigned severity of High, that Quarry, in its judgment, considers applicable to the Client Product;
- evidence of active compromise, unauthorized access, malware, or data exfiltration affecting the Client Product.

**6.3 Periodic Maintenance.** “Periodic Maintenance” is all other maintenance, including: High-rated vulnerabilities that Quarry does not consider applicable to the Client Product; Medium and Low vulnerabilities; routine updates to frameworks, languages, runtimes, libraries, and packages; server, operating system, and distribution upgrades, including upgrades ahead of end-of-life; CMS, commerce platform, and application version upgrades; vendor patches rated below Critical; TLS, cipher, and configuration hardening; and backup, logging, and access-control improvements.

**6.4 Standing authorization for Critical Remediation Events.** Client authorizes Quarry, without further approval, to use good faith and commercially reasonable efforts to remediate a Critical Remediation Event as soon as practicable after Quarry becomes aware of it. The authorization covers applying patches, upgrading affected components, changing configurations, rotating credentials, disabling affected features, taking affected systems offline, and restoring from backups, as Quarry judges necessary to contain the risk. After acting, Quarry will notify Client of the event and the action taken, normally within one business day; late or missed notice does not make Quarry liable for the event. Time spent is billed at the Rates; the rush rate under Section 3.4 applies to work Quarry reasonably determines must be performed outside business hours to contain the risk. The authorization applies once Quarry has actual knowledge of an event; Quarry has no duty to look for them. Quarry’s determinations under Sections 6.2 through 6.4, or the absence of one, are not a basis for any claim against Quarry. Client accepts the disruption, downtime, and data loss that actions under this Section may cause, and Quarry is not liable for them except to the extent of its gross negligence or willful misconduct. Client will tell Quarry in writing of any insurer, incident-response, or evidence-preservation requirements that apply to it, and is responsible for notifying its own insurers. Quarry preserves available logs where practicable.

**6.5 Withdrawing the standing authorization.** Client may withdraw the authorization in Section 6.4, in whole or for named systems, only by written notice signed by an officer or owner of Client. From the effective date of that notice, Critical Remediation Events on the excluded systems are treated as Periodic Maintenance and Sections 6.7 and 6.8 apply to them.
