# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Quarry is an elite, globally-distributed team. We do our best work when everyone — employees, collaborators, and contributors — can participate in an environment that is respectful, professional, and free of harassment, regardless of background, identity, geography, or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect, professionalism, and good faith.
- Critique ideas and code, not people; assume competence and positive intent.
- Be clear, considerate, and constructive in reviews and discussion.
- Respect differing viewpoints, time zones, and working styles.
- Accept feedback gracefully and extend the same care when giving it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning, exclusionary, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, and any communication channel associated with the project — and whenever an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns confidentially to **conduct@quarry.team**. Reports will be reviewed and handled discreetly and in good faith.

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding this Code and may take any action they deem appropriate — from a warning to removal from project spaces — in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code in good faith may face repercussions as determined by Quarry leadership.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is informed by the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), adapted for Quarry.
