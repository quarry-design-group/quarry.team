## The Challenge

The client’s search-as-a-service vendor was acquired by a competitor, and the client was required to migrate to a new platform. This presented several challenges:

- Mandatory migration with potential disruption to business operations
- Limited timeframe to make a decision and implement changes
- Need to justify any additional costs or efforts beyond the required migration

## The Solution

Quarry’s team saw this mandatory migration as a great opportunity to enhance the client’s search capabilities.

Acting as a fractional CTO, Quarry conducted a comprehensive cost-benefit analysis and business case for migrating to Algolia.

We proposed Algolia as a superior alternative to both the incumbent vendor and its acquirer; after getting buy-in, we implemented the migration, which required similar effort to the originally mandated one.

Along the way, Quarry implemented event collection to power Algolia’s advanced machine learning capabilities as an added bonus.

## The Results

The migration to Algolia delivered significant improvements across multiple areas:

**Better admin experience** – Dramatically improved admin experience enabled self-service content management in Algolia, eliminating the frequent vendor support required with the previous vendor.

**Lower costs** – Even with superior functionality, Algolia provided a more cost-effective solution.

**Improved search merchandising** – More relevant search results and improved ability to fine-tune and customize search outcomes.

**Better recommendations and personalization** – This is one of the biggest wins. We leveraged Algolia’s machine learning capabilities for better product recommendations and improved personalization without requiring constant manual intervention.

The client gained access to a powerful machine learning engine for both search and recommendations, which allowed the client to “outsource” complex optimization tasks to Algolia’s algorithms.

By acting as a fractional CTO, thinking strategically and looking beyond the immediate requirement, Quarry transformed a potential inconvenience into a significant upgrade for our client.

The migration to Algolia solved the immediate need and provided the client with a best-in-class search solution that improved user experience, reduced costs, and set the stage for future growth.
